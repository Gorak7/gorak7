# Ceiling fan safety and care

Disconnect electrical power at the breaker before cleaning or servicing a ceiling fan. Never spray liquid directly into the motor housing. Use a dry or slightly damp cloth on external surfaces and allow the fan to dry before restoring power.

If the fan wobbles, turn it off and inspect that blades are securely attached and balanced. Do not bend blades to correct wobble. Stop using the fan and contact a qualified electrician if wiring is damaged, burning smells occur, or unusual electrical noise is present.
