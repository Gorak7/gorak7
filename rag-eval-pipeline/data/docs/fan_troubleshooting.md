# Ceiling fan troubleshooting FAQ

If the fan does not start, first check the circuit breaker and wall switch. For a remote-controlled model, replace the remote batteries and check the receiver pairing instructions in the product manual.

If the fan runs slowly, confirm the selected speed and check whether the blades are obstructed. If the fan makes unusual grinding or electrical noises, switch it off and request qualified service.

The supplied project documents do not specify a warranty period, electrical rating, installation torque, or model-specific repair procedure. The assistant must say when information is unavailable rather than inventing it.
