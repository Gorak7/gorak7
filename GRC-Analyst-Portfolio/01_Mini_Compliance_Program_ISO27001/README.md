# Mini Compliance Program – ISO 27001:2022

Scenario-based baseline assessment for fictional NovaCloud AI Solutions.

## Deliverables
- `ASSESSMENT_NOTES.md`: assessment basis and conclusion.
- `Compliance_Matrix.csv`: 15 selected Annex A controls and evidence status.
- `Gap_Action_Plan.csv`: remediation actions.
- `ISO27001_Mini_Compliance_Program.xlsx`: editable asset inventory, compliance matrix, gap plan and management summary.
- `Management_Compliance_Summary.docx`: editable management report.

## Outcome
All 15 selected controls are marked Planned / not evidenced because no internal policies, access exports, tickets, configuration screenshots or audit records were supplied. This is an evidence-readiness tabletop baseline, not proof that a real company lacks controls.

> Sample mapping only; not a complete Statement of Applicability, certification, legal opinion or independent audit.
